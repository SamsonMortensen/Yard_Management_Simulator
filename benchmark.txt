
  Empirical Benchmark: Concurrency Guards vs Unsafe Baseline

  Operational Mode      FIFO?  Park Writes  Double-Parks  Conflicts Intercepted
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Dispatch (pre-claim)   Yes       60.0          0.0          0.0 (0 Conflicts)
  ==============================================================================

  Analytical Findings:
  1. The Unsafe control mode proves concurrency guards are load-bearing: without them,
     two workers produce 47.9 duplicate park writes across 60 containers.
  2. Under Guarded FIFO, the conflict count is not mere overhead: it is the exact
     count of physical double-parks intercepted and prevented by DynamoDB.
  3. Centralized Dispatch moves contention off the expensive physical drive path
     onto the in-memory claim retry, achieving 0 parking collisions while preserving FIFO.
